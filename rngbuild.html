WASLAND

Waslandian character

This was a planned feature as a potential Wasland update.

Please sit back and take a 3 second silent prayer for the lost.

It would take your ROBLOX UserID and generate a unique character based on that, similar to Rust's character system.
You then on would have been able to:

  • Change your hair at the barbershop (cost a quarter)
  • Change your name at the Javna Uprava (unfinished building in Novo Morje)
  • Completely remake your character at either there or the hospital
  • The clothes you would've bought would have been saved on your character
  • and more on https://trello.com/c/Lh6c6woi/75
